Regex to convert lines like the following into a curl command. Handles URL encoded URLs.
<tr><td valign="top"><img src="/icons/compressed.gif" alt="[   ]"></td><td><a href="Defender%20(1983)(Atarisoft)%20%5bkfx%5d%5b160k%5d%5bcp%5d%5b!%5d.zip">Defender (1983)(Atarisoft) [kfx][160k][cp][!].zip</a></td><td align="right">2024-06-21 20:24  </td><td align="right">1.7M</td><td>&nbsp;</td></tr>

------------------------------------------------------------


^<tr>.+?<a href="(.+?)">(.+?)</a>.+?</tr>$


echo '\\n\2'; curl -o '\2' "\${base_url}\1"

------------------------------------------------------------
